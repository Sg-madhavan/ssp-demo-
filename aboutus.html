About us

SanSphere Pharma is a generic pharmaceutical company focused on developing, manufacturing and sourcing complex generic pharmaceutical products, with a specific emphasis on Complex Injectables, Ophthalmic, Non-Solid & Solid Oral products.

SanSphere has created a unique portfolio of complex generics by product selection, and execution through a network of contract R&D laboratories and manufacturing organizations, that carries talented scientist and extensive development capabilities, with state of art manufacturing and innovative drug delivery technologies.

What we do

Sourcing the accurate API, developing a robust and efficient manufacturing process for complex formulations, designing a drug delivery systems without compromise on quality/ compliance, justifying in-vitro bio-equivalence study approaches in view of complex BA/BE/PK studies, proactively addressing regulatory requirements during the original dossier submission to ensure first cycle approval and evaluating market dynamics to finalize the commercial strategy for a product are some value add that we bring to our partners.

More importantly, first review cycle product approval and commercial launch to ensure our partners are quicker to market than most competitors, thereby improving access of these drugs to patients at the right cost is what truly set apart SanSphere.

Our Infrastructure

We are partnered with a state-of-the-art Center of Excellence in the heart of Hyderabad, Telangana can cater to the development of essentially all pharmaceutical dosage forms from simple oral solids/ solution/ suspensions and soft-gels to complex parenteral suspensions/ emulsions, Long-acting depot injections, complex ophthalmic suspensions/ emulsions, respiratory products and niche drug-device combination products.

This facility houses some of the most advanced formulation development and analytical characterization equipment’s to aid our team in their efforts of timely development and commercialization of products with varying degree of complexity.
