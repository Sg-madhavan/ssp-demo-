Generic Pharmaceutical Products

Complex Injectable Dosage

SanSphere team is expertise in developing complex parenteral products formulations that contains either complex ingredients or API, complex formulation, i.e., delivery carrier, complex route of administration, complex dosage form, or complex drug device combination. The complex injectable products include liposomes, microspheres, micelles and submicron crystals in suspensions, and emulsions.

Ophthalmic Dosage

SanSphere team is expertise in developing ophthalmic formulation in the form of a solution, a suspension, an ointment or an emulsion with carefully controlled pH, buffer capacity, viscosity, and tonicity. One of the most popular forms of ophthalmic formulation developed is topical instillation: a solution that enables the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) to be administered directly onto the surface of the eye.

Semi-Solid Dosage

SanSphere team is expertise in developing semisolid dosage forms like gel, cream, lotion, and ointments that are useful for topical delivery of nanocrystals. The team is specialized in nanocrystals-based topical formulations contain stabilizers, gelling agents, emulsifying agents, preservatives, antioxidants, and humectants.

Solid and Liquid Oral Dosage

SanSphere team is expertise in developing Oral solid dosage forms, including tablets (immediate, sustained, delayed and extended release), Multi unit particulate systems (MUPS), buccal and sublingual tablets, chewable tablets, capsules (Hard and Soft gelatin), solution, suspension. Powder for oral solution and powder for oral suspension (Delayed release).
